# Human validation

`two_annotator/` holds the validation used in the paper: the annotation protocol, both annotators' completed sheets (A. H. Afridi, F. A. Khan) with evidence links, agreement before adjudication (`agreement_pre_adjudication.csv`), the adjudicated disagreements (`Adjudication_Final.xlsx`) and the final labels joined to sample strata (`final_labels_films.csv`, `final_labels_products.csv`).

`superseded/` holds two earlier single-annotator passes made before the protocol was written. They are kept for transparency and are not used in any result.
